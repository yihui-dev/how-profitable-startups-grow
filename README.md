## FAQ

### Where does the data come from?

Revenue data is compiled from publicly shared information online and shown here only as a band. Builders and growth questions come from BizPeel’s research on publicly shared sources, and each full report cites its sources.

### How often is it updated?

Every Monday. The “data as of” badge shows the date of the newest revenue data in the tables.

### How do I get a startup listed?

Every startup with a published report on BizPeel appears here from the next weekly update. To suggest a startup, [contact BizPeel](https://bizpeel.com/contact?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=how-profitable-startups-grow&utm_content=faq).

### Why no exact numbers?

The tables are a map of where to look. Exact MRR, growth and traffic, with the evidence behind them, are in each startup’s report on BizPeel, where every figure carries its source and date.
